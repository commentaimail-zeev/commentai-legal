CommentAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CommentAI ("CommentAI", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when businesses use the CommentAI service and connect supported third-party platforms.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, CommentAI may process information such as:

  • Business account information, including business name and account details.
  • User account information, including email address and authentication information.
  • Identifiers associated with connected social media or business accounts.
  • Authorization information required to connect supported third-party platforms.
  • Public customer interactions such as comments, reviews, and related conversation context.
  • Customer names, usernames, or platform identifiers made available through connected platforms.
  • Business instructions, settings, and information provided to help CommentAI generate appropriate responses.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Connect and operate supported social and business platforms.
  • Retrieve and process customer interactions authorized by the business.
  • Generate, review, and manage suggested or automated responses.
  • Maintain conversation context and prevent duplicate processing.
  • Apply business preferences, instructions, and approved business information.
  • Provide security, authentication, troubleshooting, and service functionality.
  • Improve the reliability and performance of CommentAI.

3. Third-Party Platforms and Service Providers

CommentAI may connect with third-party platforms such as Meta services, including Instagram and Facebook, when authorized by the business. Information received from these platforms is processed according to the permissions granted by the business and the applicable platform policies.

CommentAI may also use trusted technology service providers, including infrastructure, database, security, and artificial intelligence providers, when necessary to operate the service.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Platform Authorization Data

When a business connects a supported platform, CommentAI may receive authorization credentials or access tokens required to provide the requested integration. These credentials are used only for authorized service functions and are protected using appropriate security measures.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and operational records, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve legitimate business or technical issues.

Businesses may request deletion of their CommentAI account information and associated data, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Sharing

We may share information only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and operate CommentAI.
  • Work with service providers acting on our behalf.
  • Comply with applicable law or valid legal requirements.
  • Protect the security, rights, and integrity of CommentAI, our users, or others.
