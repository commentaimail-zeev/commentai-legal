CommentAI Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Users and businesses may request deletion of personal information and account data associated with CommentAI.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to: commentaimail@gmail.com

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your CommentAI account.
  • The name of the business associated with the account, if applicable.
  • A clear statement that you are requesting deletion of your CommentAI data.

We may need to verify that the requester is authorized to request deletion for the relevant account or business before completing the request.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the account and services used, deletion may include information such as:

  • CommentAI account information.
  • Business settings and preferences.
  • Connected platform account information.
  • Stored authorization data associated with disconnected accounts.
  • Customer interaction data stored by CommentAI.
  • Other data associated with the CommentAI account, where applicable.

Connected Third-Party Platforms

Deleting information from CommentAI does not automatically delete information stored independently by third-party platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, or other connected services.

Users may also remove CommentAI's access through the settings provided by the relevant third-party platform.

Retention Requirements

Certain limited information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational requirements.

Contact

For questions regarding deletion requests, contact: commentaimail@gmail.com
